## The Short Version

We don't collect anything about you. No accounts. No data. No tracking. No internet required. That's it.

---

## 1. Who We Are

We develop and publish the mobile word game _Wordle_. This policy explains what data we handle — which is essentially none.

---

## 2. No Registration, No Accounts

The App does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information whatsoever to use any feature.

---

## 3. No Data Collection or Storage

We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data. Specifically:

- We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any other identifier.
- We do not store game history, scores, guesses, or any gameplay data — on your device or on any server.
- We have no database. There is no backend storage of any kind.
- We do not use cookies or any equivalent tracking technology.
- We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data.
- We do not use analytics SDKs or crash-reporting tools that transmit personal data.

---

## 4. Fully Offline — No Internet Required

The App works entirely on your device. It does not require an internet connection to function, and it does not make network requests of any kind while you play. No data is sent to us or to any third party during use of the App.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Because we collect no personal data from any user, the App is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions), and in fact collect no information from anyone at all.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, please contact us — though there is no personal data for us to access or delete.

---

## 6. Third-Party Links or Services

The App contains no links to third-party websites and integrates no third-party SDKs that collect personal data. Because the App operates fully offline, it does not connect to external services.

---

## 7. Data Security

Since we collect no data, there is no personal data for us to secure or for anyone to breach.

## 8. Your Rights

Under laws such as GDPR, CCPA, and others, you may have rights regarding your personal data (access, deletion, portability, etc.). Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide, delete, or transfer. If you have questions, you're welcome to contact us and we will confirm this.
